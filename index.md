# Privacy Policy for BALLROWS

**Last Updated:** March 26, 2026

Welcome to BALLROWS ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Collected Automatically
When you use the App, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the App, including session duration, levels completed, high scores, and crash reports.

### B. App Tracking Transparency and Advertisements
We use third-party advertising partners, specifically Google AdMob, to serve advertisements within the App. Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, we explicitly request your permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising.
- **If you opt-in:** Our advertising partners will collect your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads and measure engagement.
- **If you opt-out:** You will still see advertisements, but they will be less relevant to your interests. Your device will not be tracked using the IDFA.

### C. Game Center Information
To provide player identity and cloud syncing, the App integrates with Apple's Game Center. We do not store your Game Center data on external servers; all gameplay interactions sync natively with iCloud and Apple Game Center APIs according to Apple's privacy guidelines.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To operate, maintain, and improve the App's features and performance.
- To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob (based on your ATT preferences).
- To monitor gameplay analytics and fix bugs.
- To save and sync your custom player profile and game progress.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:
- **Google AdMob:** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Apple Game Center & iCloud:** [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 4. Data Retention and Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information. We do not store personal data on proprietary external servers. Any gameplay data saved locally is kept on your device or securely backed up via your personal iCloud account. Advertising partner data is retained according to their specific privacy policies.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately to have it deleted.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@ballrows.com
